❓ How to use

  • Station tab: create a station per sounding point, capture GPS, add a voice note or photo.
  • Sounding tab: pick your array, enter spacings + the resistance reading (ΔV/I). K and ρₐ compute automatically. Red rows = suspicious readings — re-sound those points.
  • Curve & AI tab: watch your log–log curve grow, read the AI's plain-language take on what the ground is doing, and check the Pro recommendation — a data-quality grade plus a clear drill / extend / resurvey verdict.
  • Map tab: see all your stations plotted from their GPS tags.
  • Report tab: export CSV for your spreadsheet, or a Word-ready HTML report for the client file.
  • Everything is stored on this device and works fully offline. Back up with the JSON export before clearing browser data.
